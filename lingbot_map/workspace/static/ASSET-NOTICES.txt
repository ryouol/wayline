Wayline presentation assets — September 11, 2026

The public office example and source-frame thumbnails are derived from the
first 10 seconds of TUM RGB-D Benchmark freiburg3_long_office_household.
Attribution: J. Sturm, N. Engelhard, F. Endres, W. Burgard and D. Cremers,
“A Benchmark for the Evaluation of RGB-D SLAM Systems,” IROS 2012.
Dataset: https://cvg.cit.tum.de/data/datasets/rgbd-dataset
Data license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Modifications: video frames reconstructed through LingBot-Map, coordinate
basis corrected for display, point cloud reduced to 75,000 points for the
landing animation, and light/dark views captured with the Wayline renderer.
Studio images show the same 750,000-point reference in an isolated local
workspace. No private user capture is included in these public assets.
No endorsement by TUM or the dataset authors is implied.

Dataset permission does not establish model/checkpoint/output clearance.
The reconstruction retains NOASSERTION output metadata and the product's
research-only policy. See MODEL_PROVENANCE.md in the project repository.

The Wayline two-plane W mark was generated for this redesign with OpenAI
ImageGen. The mark is original product artwork, not an Apple mark.
Favicon/manifest sizes and WebP delivery variants are offline exports of this
approved artwork and the actual viewer captures, made with ImageMagick 7 using
scripts/export_web_assets.sh. They do not redraw or alter the scene content.
